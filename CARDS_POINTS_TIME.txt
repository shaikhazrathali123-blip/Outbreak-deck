================================================================
     OUTBREAK DECK  -  CARDS, POINTS AND TIME  (rules v3.0)
================================================================

A short guide to four questions:
  1. Who gets a Gun?
  2. Who gets a Zombie card?
  3. Who gets a Cure?
  4. How do points and time work?

The full rulebook is GAME_GUIDE.txt. If this guide and the
rulebook ever disagree, the rulebook wins.


----------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHO IS WHO AT THE START
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Everyone gets 7 cards. A few players are secretly ZOMBIES:

    Players in the match    Zombies at the start
    6 to 8                  1
    9 to 12                 2
    13 to 15                3

  Everyone else is HUMAN.


----------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHO GETS A GUN
----------------------------------------------------------------

  AT THE START
    Some humans start with a Gun (rounded). v3: it depends on the
    lobby size (balance, tuned with the simulator):

      Players   Share of humans   Example
      6         50%               5 humans  -> 3 Guns
      7-8       20%               7 humans  -> 1 Gun
      9-12      40%               10 humans -> 4 Guns
      13-15     45%               12 humans -> 5 Guns

    Double Guns modifier: twice the share.
    Who gets one is random. An armed human gets the Gun + 6 cards.
    The other humans start WITHOUT a Gun.

  LATER IN THE MATCH
    - At a refill point (only when your hand is empty) you may
      draw a Gun from the deck.
    - If a human holding a Gun is BITTEN, the Gun goes in secret
      to a random human who holds no Gun and no Cure.
    - LAST STAND: the last human gets a 1-bullet Gun (or one more
      bullet), even next to a Cure.

  LIMITS
    - Only humans. Zombies NEVER hold a Gun.
    - A human holds ONE Gun or ONE Cure, never both (Last Stand is
      the one exception).
    - A Gun has 2 bullets. After the 2nd bullet it is used up.


----------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHO GETS A ZOMBIE CARD
----------------------------------------------------------------

  - Only zombies. Zombie cards are never in the deck.
  - The first zombies start with 1 Zombie card.
  - A zombie holds ONE Zombie card at most.
  - After you play it, a new one grows back after ONE fixed time
    for the lobby (v3, no more "+20s per zombie"):

      Players   Regrow
      6         75s
      7-8       45s
      9-12      65s
      13-15     95s

    Fast Claws modifier: 25% faster.
  - A newly bitten zombie gets their first one the same time after
    turning.
  - A bluff call that names your Zombie card spends it: it regrows.
  - A cured zombie loses their Zombie card.


----------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHO GETS A CURE
----------------------------------------------------------------

  - Only humans. Zombies NEVER hold a Cure.
  - Cures are in the deck (rare; Extra Cures modifier: twice as
    many). A human who did not start with a Gun may be dealt one at
    the start, or draw one at a refill.
  - A human holds ONE Gun or ONE Cure, never both.
  - If a human holding a Cure is BITTEN, the Cure goes in secret to
    a random human who holds no Gun and no Cure.
  - A Cure only works when you PLAY it on a zombie. It never
    protects you. You can't cure yourself.


----------------------------------------------------------------
5. POINTS (secret)
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Your TEAM still wins or loses the match. Points decide the
  TOP PLAYER (the gold star on the results screen).

  Only you see your own score (the gold chip under your role).
  Everyone's score is revealed at the end. Tap the (i) next to a
  player on the results screen to see where their points came from.

  YOUR SCORE = CARD POINTS + EARNED POINTS + TIME BONUS

  CARD POINTS (only while the card is in your hand)
    Number card     its number (1 to 9)
    Gun             10 for each bullet left (full Gun = 20)
    Cure            30
    Zombie card      0

  EARNED POINTS
    You shoot a ZOMBIE                 +40   (every shot)
    You shoot a HUMAN                  -20
    Your Zombie card bites             +15
    You cure a zombie                  -30   (the Cure is used up)
    You waste a Cure                   -40   (played on a human)
    v3:
    Win a number vs number duel         +5
    Fit a generator part               +10
    Your vote helped your team         +20   (you voted for the player
    Your vote hurt your team           -10    who was voted out)
    Right bluff call                   +15
    Wrong bluff call                   -10
    End: zombie still in, never
         accused in a meeting          +25
    End: you were the Last Stand
         human and made it             +50

  EXAMPLE: you hold 7, 4, 2 and a full Gun = 33 points.
    Shoot a zombie          -> 73
    Shoot another zombie    -> 113
    Shoot a human instead   -> 13
    Never shoot             -> stays 33

  DUELS
    Win a number duel -> you take the loser's card, its points, +5.
    Lose a number duel -> the card you played is gone, and its points.
    (Bitten by a Zombie card: you keep the number card you played.)

  WHEN YOU ARE OUT
    Shot or voted out -> your score freezes at what it was.

  XP AND RANKS (v3, saved on this device)
    XP per match = your score (at least 0) + 50 for a win.
    Rookie 0 · Orderly 300 · Researcher 800 · Specialist 1600 ·
    Director 3000. Each rank unlocks 3 more neon colours.


----------------------------------------------------------------
6. TIME BONUS
----------------------------------------------------------------

  +10 points for every FULL MINUTE you stay in your role
  (human or zombie).

  Your role changes -> the time bonus drops back to 0 and starts
  again:
    - a human who is BITTEN loses their human time bonus,
    - a zombie who is CURED loses their zombie time bonus.

  Meetings pause the time bonus, like the match clock.

  Example: 3 minutes as a human = +30. Bitten -> 0.
           1 minute later as a zombie = +10.


----------------------------------------------------------------
7. ALL THE TIMERS
----------------------------------------------------------------

  THE MATCH
    Match length               4, 6 or 8 minutes (menu)
    At 0:00                    the bigger team wins (tie = humans)
    Lightning                  every 90s: all rooms lit for a moment

  ZOMBIES
    New Zombie card            one regrow time per lobby (section 3)
    First card after turning   the same
    Hunger                     20s holding a Zombie card unused
                               -> STARVING (no card = no hunger)
    Starving                   growl + green flare every 20s
    Blackout                   once per match, 15s, not before 45s

  GUNS AND CURES
    Shooter stunned            none (v3)
    Cure user in the cell      30s (can't move, duel, chat or vote)
    Cured player gagged        30s (no chat, no vote)
    Cured player recovering    20s (can't be bitten)

  POWER AND EVAC
    Pick up a part             stand still 4s
    Fit a part                 stand still 4s at the generator
    Evac truck                 90s after the 4th part
    Security cameras           10s between looks

  DUELS AND CHASES
    Accept or run              5s
    Talk at the table          10s (voice or message, before cards;
                               online play only)
    Pick a card                5s  (bluff call before you pick)
    Duel cooldown              10s
    Zombie beaten by a number  stunned 12s
    Chase                      8s to touch the runner
    Refill                     stand still 5s at a refill point
                               (only with an empty hand: back to 4)

  MEETINGS
    First meeting              not before 30s (body reports too)
    Between meetings           30s
    Talk / vote                30s / 15s
    Meetings per player        1 (body reports don't use it)
