================================================================
                OUTBREAK DECK  -  HOW TO PLAY  (v2.3)
================================================================

THE IDEA IN ONE LINE
--------------------
You are trapped in the Helix research facility. A few players are
secretly ZOMBIES (infected). Everyone fights with cards. If a zombie
plays its ZOMBIE card on you, you become a zombie too.
Trust no one.


----------------------------------------------------------------
1. TEAMS
----------------------------------------------------------------

At the start the game secretly gives everyone a role. Only YOU can
see your own role.

  HUMAN
    - Most players are human.
    - Only humans can carry a GUN or a CURE. Zombies can never hold
      them (see "LOSING GUNS AND CURES" in section 3).
    - A human can hold only ONE of them: a Gun OR a Cure, never both,
      and never two of the same.

  INFECTED (ZOMBIE)
    - A few players start infected:
        6-8 players   -> 1 infected
        9-12 players  -> 2 infected
        13-15 players -> 3 infected
    - Infected players can see each other (green outline).
    - Two infected CAN duel each other: it is a FAKE DUEL. It looks
      like a normal duel to everyone else, but both keep their cards.
    - The first infected player is called "PATIENT ZERO".

  A human hit by a ZOMBIE card is BITTEN and TURNS INTO A ZOMBIE
  AT ONCE. There is no waiting time (see section 6).


----------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW TO WIN
----------------------------------------------------------------

  BEFORE THE TIMER ENDS
    HUMANS WIN   if every zombie has been shot, voted out, or cured.
    ZOMBIES WIN  if every human has turned, been shot or voted out.

  WHEN THE TIMER ENDS, THE BIGGER TEAM WINS
    - More zombies than humans left  -> ZOMBIES WIN.
    - More humans than zombies left  -> HUMANS WIN.
    - Exactly the same number        -> HUMANS WIN (they held out).
    Shot and voted-out players do not count.

  A human in a temporary CURE QUARANTINE (section 3) still counts
  as a human for winning.

  Match length: 4, 6 or 8 minutes (choose in the menu).

  SECRET POINTS: THE TOP PLAYER
    Your TEAM wins or loses the match. On top of that, every player
    has a secret SCORE, and the highest score is the TOP PLAYER.
    Only you can see your own score (the gold chip under your role).
    Nobody can see anyone else's. All scores are revealed at the end.

    Your score = the points of the cards in your hand
               + the points you banked.

    CARD POINTS (count only while the card is in your hand)
      Number card   its number (1-9)
      Gun           20  (while it still has bullets)
      Cure          30
      Zombie card    0

    BANKED POINTS (yours to keep)
      Your Gun shoots a zombie   +10
      Your Gun shoots a human    -10
      Your Zombie card bites     +15

    TIME BONUS (live)
      +10 for every full minute you stay in your role (human or
      zombie). If your role changes (you get bitten, or you are
      cured) the time bonus drops back to 0 and starts again.
      Meetings pause it, like the match clock.

    WHAT IT COSTS
      Lose a duel   -> the card you played goes, and its points too.
      Cure a zombie -> you save them, but the Cure is gone: -30.
      Cure a human  -> wasted: the Cure is gone (-30) and -10 more = -40.
      Last bullet   -> the empty Gun leaves your hand: -20.
      Bitten        -> your Gun or Cure is handed off, and its points.
      Shot or voted out -> your score freezes at what it was.

    Ties share the top spot.


----------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE CARDS  (only 4 kinds)
----------------------------------------------------------------

  1 - 9    NUMBER CARDS
           Your power in a duel. Higher number wins. That's it.

  ZOMBIE   (only zombies have it)
           Beats ANY number card, even a 9, and BITES the loser.
           The loser turns into a zombie IMMEDIATELY.
           A zombie holds only ONE Zombie card. It is used up when
           played, and a new one grows 40 SECONDS later, so a zombie
           can bite at most once every 40 seconds.
           The first zombies start with their card. A newly bitten
           zombie grows their first card 40 seconds after turning.

  GUN      (rare, humans only; you hold a Gun OR a Cure, never both)
           A Gun has 2 BULLETS. Each time you play it, one bullet is
           used. When both are gone, the card is used up.
           The Gun KILLS whoever you play it on, human or zombie:
             - Gun vs ZOMBIE card   -> the zombie is dead.
             - Gun vs number card   -> that player is dead, human or
                                       zombie.
           A dead player is out of the match. Their role stays SECRET.
           EVERY SHOT IS ANNOUNCED to everyone (for example "P5 shot
           P3"): the names, but NEVER the target's role.
           After any shot, the shooter is STUNNED for 8 seconds.
           Zombies can never hold a Gun.
           Killing a human helps the zombies. Only shoot when sure!

  CURE     (rare, humans only; you hold a Gun OR a Cure, never both)
           You must PLAY it in a duel against a zombie:
                - Cure vs ZOMBIE card: you are not bitten, the Cure is
                  used up, and the zombie is CURED (becomes human).
                - Cure vs number card, opponent is a zombie: the zombie
                  is cured, but they win that duel with their number
                  (no bite, since they played a number).
                - Cure vs number card, opponent is human: the Cure is
                  WASTED and you lose the duel. Nothing else happens.
           You CANNOT use a Cure on yourself. Holding one does NOT
           protect you: if a Zombie card bites you, you turn like
           anyone else, and your Cure is passed on (see below).

           WHEN A ZOMBIE IS CURED:
             - They become human, lose all Zombie cards, and hunger
               stops.
             - They forget the other zombies (green outlines gone).
             - They are GAGGED for 60 seconds (no quick-chat, no
               accusing, no voting).
             - They are "RECOVERING" for 20 seconds (pale tint) and
               cannot be bitten.
             - They are not told who cured them.

           THE CURE USER IS QUARANTINED FOR 60 SECONDS:
             - They go to the quarantine cell and cannot move, duel,
               or call a meeting.
             - They are gagged: no quick-chat, no accusing, no voting,
               even during a meeting.
             - They cannot be bitten or voted out while inside.
             - The timer pauses during meetings.
             - Everyone sees "a player was quarantined", with no reason.
             - Wasted Cure (opponent was human): NO quarantine.

  LOSING GUNS AND CURES (when a holder is bitten):
           The moment a human is BITTEN, they turn and every Gun and
           Cure they hold is DISCARDED. They can never use them as a
           zombie.
           Each discarded card then goes to a RANDOM other human who is
           still in the match and not a zombie.
             - NO announcement and NO hint: nobody is told that a card
               was discarded, who lost it, or who received it. The new
               owner just finds it in their hand.
             - It only goes to a human who holds NO Gun and NO Cure
               (one of them per player).
             - If no other human can receive it, the card goes back to
               the refill supply.

  Quick table:
     number vs number  ->  higher number wins (same number: draw)
     ZOMBIE vs number  ->  ZOMBIE wins, the human turns at once
     GUN    vs ZOMBIE  ->  GUN wins, the zombie is dead
     GUN    vs number  ->  GUN wins, that player is dead
     GUN    vs CURE    ->  GUN wins, the Cure player is dead
     CURE   vs ZOMBIE  ->  no bite, zombie cured, Cure user quarantined
     CURE   vs number  ->  zombie: cured, number wins the duel
                           human: Cure wasted, you lose
     same vs same      ->  draw, cards go back
                           (GUN vs GUN: each Gun still uses a bullet)


----------------------------------------------------------------
4. DUELS
----------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1 - CHALLENGE
   Walk close to another player. The CHALLENGE button appears.
   Press it (or E).

STEP 2 - ACCEPT OR RUN
   The other player has 5 seconds to choose:
     ACCEPT (Y)  -> both walk to the nearest duel table.
     RUN    (N)  -> a CHASE starts (see below).
   If they do not choose in time, they RUN.

STEP 3 - PICK A CARD
   At the table you have 5 seconds to pick one card.
   (If you do not pick, a random card is played for you.)
   Both cards flip over at the same time.

STEP 4 - RESULT
   - Number win: the winner takes the loser's card.
   - Zombie win: the loser is bitten and turns at once (a Cure in
     their hand does not help).
   - Gun: the target is shot and is out of the match; the shot is
     announced and the shooter is stunned for 8 seconds.
   - Cure: on a zombie, the zombie is cured and the Cure user is
     quarantined for 60 seconds. On a human, the Cure is wasted and
     you lose the duel (see section 3).
   - A human who beats a zombie with a NUMBER card also gets a CLUE,
     and the zombie is STUNNED for 12 seconds (cannot move or duel).
   After a duel, both players wait 10 seconds before they can duel again.


----------------------------------------------------------------
5. THE CHASE  (when someone runs)
----------------------------------------------------------------

   - The runner gets a short head start (about 1 second).
   - Then the challenger has 8 SECONDS to catch them.
   - The chaser is a little faster than the runner.
   - If the chaser TOUCHES the runner, the duel is forced: no more
     running.
   - If the runner gets away (climbing a ladder helps!), the chase
     ends and nobody can challenge either of them for a few seconds.

   When YOU are being chased, the screen pulses red like a heartbeat.


----------------------------------------------------------------
6. BITTEN: YOU TURN AT ONCE
----------------------------------------------------------------

   When a Zombie card beats you, you are BITTEN and turn into a
   zombie right away. Your role chip changes to ZOMBIE, you see the
   other zombies (green outline), and your first Zombie card
   grows 40 seconds later.
   Nobody else is told. They only find out by watching you.

   Holding a CURE does not protect you: you cannot cure yourself.
   The only way to stop a bite is to PLAY your Cure in that duel
   (Cure vs ZOMBIE card). Any Gun or Cure you hold when bitten is
   discarded and given secretly to a random human (section 3).

   Your hunger clock starts at the moment you turn (section 7).

----------------------------------------------------------------
7. HUNGER (zombies)
----------------------------------------------------------------

   A zombie must bite someone at least every 60 SECONDS (1 minute).
   If not, they start STARVING:
     - every 40 seconds they flare green for a moment,
     - a hungry growl from their room is announced every 40 seconds.
   A bite feeds them and resets the clock.


----------------------------------------------------------------
8. EMERGENCY MEETINGS AND VOTING
----------------------------------------------------------------

   The red EMERGENCY BUTTON is in the CENTRAL HALL (floor 2).
     - Each player can call ONE meeting per match.
     - No meeting in the first 30 seconds, or within 30 seconds of the
       last one.
   Everyone still in the match is pulled into the hall (Cure users
   stay in the quarantine cell). The match clock PAUSES, and so do
   hunger, quarantine, gag and recovering timers, cooldowns and
   stuns.

   1. DISCUSS (30 seconds): tap a player, then a quick-chat button:
        ACCUSE, VOUCH, I'M CLEAN, SKIP.
      Anyone can lie. "I'M CLEAN" is only a claim now: nothing can
      prove it.
   2. VOTE (15 seconds): tap a player, then VOTE. Or SKIP VOTE.
   3. RESULT: the player with the most votes goes to the QUARANTINE
      CELL and is out of the match. Their role stays SECRET.
      A tie, or as many skips as the top votes = nobody goes.

   GAGGED PLAYERS (cure-quarantined users and freshly cured zombies)
   cannot use quick-chat or vote until their 60 seconds are over.
   A player inside the 60-second cure quarantine cannot be voted out.


----------------------------------------------------------------
9. HOW TO SPOT A ZOMBIE
----------------------------------------------------------------

   - CLUES: beat a zombie with a number card and you get a hint like
     "Your opponent's hands felt cold...". But beating a HUMAN gives a
     false hint 1 time in 8, so a clue is never proof.
   - CURE: if your Cure cures someone, they were 100% a zombie.
   - SIGNS that grow over about 60 seconds after turning:
        * a very faint green tint, and a rare split-second green flicker
        * they twitch now and then
        * they leave faintly glowing green footprints
   - STARVING zombies growl and flare green for a moment.
   - SHOTS: every shot is announced with the shooter's name (not the
     target's role). A shot at a human makes the shooter look bad.
   - A sudden "a player was quarantined" message means somebody just
     used a Cure on a zombie... or something else is going on.
   - Zombies duel each other too (fake duels), so "they never duel
     each other" is NOT a clue.


----------------------------------------------------------------
10. RUNNING OUT OF CARDS
----------------------------------------------------------------

   If you have no cards at all, you cannot challenge, and nobody can
   challenge you. (A Cure counts as a card you can play.)

   To refill: go to a CARD REFILL point and stand still for
   5 seconds. You get back up to 4 cards.
   Refill points never give a Gun or a Cure to a zombie, and never
   give a human a second one (a Gun OR a Cure, never both).

   Card refill points: SUPPLY ROOM (ground floor),
                       STORAGE (floor 3),
                       CAFETERIA (basement).

----------------------------------------------------------------
12. THE MAP
----------------------------------------------------------------

   The facility is seen from the side, like a building cut open.
   4 floors, 15 rooms. Use ladders to go up and down.

   Floor 3   | Lab 1  | Research Lab | Lab 2 | Storage    |
   Floor 2   | Medbay |     Central Hall     | Cure Lab   |
   Ground    | Entrance | Lobby | Locker Room | Supply Room |
   Basement  | Server Room | Cafeteria | Reactor | Quarantine |

   LIGHTS: only the room you are in is fully lit. Rooms next to you
   are dim. Far rooms are dark, so you cannot see who is inside.

   10 DUEL TABLES (glowing blue, marked "DUEL") on every floor.
   EMERGENCY BUTTON: Central Hall.
   QUARANTINE CELL: basement, far right. It holds two kinds of
   prisoners: voted-out players (permanent) and cure users
   (temporary, marked with a light).
   MEDBAY and CURE LAB are normal rooms now (no stations).


----------------------------------------------------------------
13. QUICK TIPS
----------------------------------------------------------------

   As a HUMAN
   - Your Gun has 2 bullets and kills anyone, human or zombie.
     Shoot only someone you are sure about.
   - You carry a Gun OR a Cure, never both. A Cure must be PLAYED
     on a zombie; it never protects you. Curing someone costs you
     60 seconds in quarantine. Use it when it is worth it.
   - Running can save your life. Head for a ladder.
   - Watch for green flickers, twitching and glowing footprints.
   - A bite turns you at once, so avoid duels with anyone you
     suspect unless you are ready to PLAY your Cure or your Gun.

   As a ZOMBIE
   - Act normal. Do not chase everyone at once.
   - Save your Zombie cards for humans you can catch.
   - If you have no Zombie card, play numbers: you can't infect,
     but a Gun can still kill you.
   - Gun holders are dangerous: a shot at you is announced.
   - If you bite a Gun or Cure holder, the card is taken away and
     secretly given to another human, so a new holder may appear
     anywhere.
   - Do not go hungry: starving zombies growl and flare green.
   - Duel your fellow zombies in front of humans. It looks normal.
   - If someone is quarantined for 60 seconds, use the time to spread.
